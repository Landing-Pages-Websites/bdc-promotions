# Direction B decomposition notes

- Build every field, rule, crop mark, folio number, index, margin note, price ledger, proof band, CTA, and FAQ in live HTML/CSS/inline SVG. Raster assets are only the customer-supplied portfolio artifacts.
- Use Cormorant Garamond and Inter exactly per the typography contract. Keep sentence-case editorial headings and compact sans utility labels.
- Color is semantic and load-bearing: cobalt marks decisions/motion, teal marks method/annotation, navy frames evidence/commitment, and warm paper carries clarity/trust. Do not flatten these roles into incidental accents.
- `01-showroom-momentum` balances a large quiet serif headline against one full documented campaign plate. The complete plate stays visible inside its cobalt frame.
- `02-operating-signal` is a compact paper index with alternating cobalt and teal folio numerals.
- `03-growth-lane` keeps margin annotations distinct from the ordered live step ledger and the navy-framed source plate.
- `04-work-in-motion` is a staggered documentary contact sheet. Preserve all four source artifacts whole, at deliberately varied scales, with live crop marks and provenance notes.
- `05-proof-with-standards` keeps broad paper quiet space above one teal methods band. No generic cards.
- `06-supported-options` is a typographic ledger, not cards. On mobile each row becomes a labeled mini-ledger while preserving service/investment/term/includes hierarchy.
- `07-clear-the-lane` is a three-column commitment chapter: statement, FAQ sequence, and navy guided-audit card. Use real links and semantic FAQ disclosures.
