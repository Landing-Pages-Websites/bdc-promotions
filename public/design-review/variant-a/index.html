# Direction A decomposition notes

- Build all surfaces, typography, buttons, dividers, proof icons, pricing bays, FAQs, and the page-long route system in live HTML/CSS/inline SVG.
- Use Barlow Condensed and Barlow exactly per the typography contract. Preserve three-line display hierarchy on narrow screens with fluid `clamp()` sizing.
- The route motif is structural, not a separate raster. Give each section its own responsive SVG segment with contracted entry/exit anchors so the path reads continuously without crossing content.
- `01-showroom-momentum` is an asymmetric overlap: copy mass left, dominant full poster right, smaller event poster intruding from center. Keep the right poster dominant and the CTA group visible in the first desktop viewport.
- `02-operating-signal` is a short strip, never a full-height section.
- `03-growth-lane` converts the horizontal staircase into a vertical ordered route on mobile. Keep all five step titles and copy together.
- `04-work-in-motion` uses four customer-supplied artifacts in one staggered portfolio rail. Preserve each whole artifact, its unique size, and role. Do not reuse any of these assets elsewhere.
- `05-proof-with-standards` is intentionally quiet. Keep large negative space and omit the reference frame's non-authoritative stock car photo rather than inventing proof.
- `06-supported-options` is the loud cobalt chapter; pricing content stays live and exact.
- `07-clear-the-lane` uses live CTA links and semantic FAQ disclosures. The route line terminates beside the audit CTA.
