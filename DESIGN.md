# BDC Promotions — homepage directions A / B / C

Pre-approval homepage review. Three routes (`/variant-a`, `/variant-b`, `/variant-c`) share
verified content only; each owns its components, CSS module, fonts and image paths.

## Design system in use

`design-psyche` (global). A and B are **locked to the supplied images** (`workflow/homepage/refs/`):
their composition, section order, palette, type roles, image subjects/crops and line breaks
come from the image, not from taste rules. Craft corrections are logged in
`workflow/homepage/DEVIATIONS.md`. C is produced by the full design-psyche loop
(axes → live references → reproduction → transplant → rank); its brief is below.

## Variant C · Proof Wall — brief (full spec: `workflow/homepage/C-BRIEF.md`)

- **Audience:** dealership GMs, general sales managers and marketing managers comparing agencies.
- **Thesis:** *the work is the proof and the price is on the wall.* The fold is a staggered
  three-column wall of all seven customer-supplied ads plus the four published price
  stickers, beside one heavy headline. Below the fold, each ad is shown whole and labelled,
  prices sit in a window-sticker ledger that ends on the non-guarantee, the path is a straight
  five-step lane ending on a showroom, and the FAQ is open.
- **Niche findings (live SERP, "car dealership marketing agency", n=9):** 0/9 show real ad
  creative in the fold; 0/9 publish prices; 7/9 use dark folds; 6/9 use logo walls or metric
  claims (forbidden here).
- **Excellence references (frozen, `workflow/homepage/c-rank/REFERENCE-SET.md`):** Superside
  (reproduced → `design-psyche/psyche/compositions/work-wall-split/`), W+K, The Mill, MVSM,
  Billion Dollar Boy, Movers+Shakers, The Social Shepherd, Ask Phill, Buck, Outpost.
- **System:**
  - Archivo at width 125, weight 800 for display, sentence case.
  - Archivo at width 100 for body; IBM Plex Mono for labels.
  - Seven type sizes site-wide.
  - Palette: concrete `#ECECE8`, paper, ink `#111214`, graphite `#1B1C1F`, one tail-light red `#C8231B`.
  - Radius 10, pill buttons, no motion, no shadows.
- **Section order:** header (graphite, phone plus audit pill) → proof-wall fold → pillar strip →
  the work (contact sheet with captions) → standards → pricing ledger → path band with a
  showroom photo → close with the audit steps and open FAQ → footer. On mobile, a fixed call
  and audit bar.
- **Images:** the seven customer ads (copied byte-identical to `public/images/design/variant-c/`)
  and the approved illustrative night-showroom image captioned "Illustrative image". No people,
  no device mockups, nothing generated.
- **Excluded:** logo walls, badges, stars, testimonials, metrics, the unverified "15 years"
  claim, dealer names in captions, price highlights, A's composite and headlight image, B's
  handwriting and crop marks.
- **How it beats A and B (checkable):**
  - The fold shows 7 pieces of work, against 2 in A and 1 in B.
  - All 4 prices appear above y=864, against about y 3,900 in A and B.
  - The wall covers at least 45% of the fold.
  - Each ad appears whole below the fold.
  - The prices end on the non-guarantee line.
