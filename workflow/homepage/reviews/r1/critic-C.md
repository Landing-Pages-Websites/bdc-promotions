# Round 1 — critic:C

## Verdict: FAIL
The most damaging problem is that the "window sticker", the one idea that makes C a direction, only exists as a name. On screen it is four white price cards with 2px black borders, stretched to fill column height, and they break the wall into a checkerboard where no ad has any impact. The rest of the chrome (mono labels, red-square eyebrows, cool grey) is the 2025 agency template. Take the customer ads out and nothing on the page belongs to BDC.

## AI-tell flags: 5
- **Rejected verdict repeated: V165 [CORRECTNESS]** — Standards section (1536 slice desktop-04, y≈797) prints "No unverified dealership count, ad-spend total, client logos, or outcome statistics." The same section also shows internal staff rules as content: "01 Show only customer-approved work and attribution" and "02 Use testimonial video only after transcript and publication approval". This is the V165 mechanism: the build's own content rules shown to the buyer as a description of what the site doesn't have. A GM reads it as "they have no clients to show." — **Fix:** delete the Standards section (`#standards`). The ledger footer already prints the verified non-guarantee, which is the V186 negation, and the Work section already proves the work is customer-supplied. This also removes the duplicate "Proof you can inspect." (Work row 3 and the Standards H2).
- **Terminal/mono look on a product that isn't a dev tool** — IBM Plex Mono sets 66 of 177 text elements (styles.json). That covers every eyebrow, the fold proof list, sticker names and terms, pillar descriptors, work captions, the ledger's Term column, the path eyebrow and the close label. None of the 10 frozen Set B references uses a monospace face (Inter Tight/Instrument Serif, Futura/Neue Haas/Editorial New, UniversusSans, Univers, Metric/Copernicus, Mabry, Inter, swizzy, scto/bradford, Arial/DIN), so the reference set cannot excuse it. — **Fix:** remove Plex Mono. Set labels in Archivo wdth 100 at 12/600 uppercase +0.06em. Set caption line 1 in Archivo 13/500 steel, sentence case. The ledger's `No.` column becomes tabular Archivo. This page has no code, so mono has no job.
- **Adjective feature row / lorem-like copy** — the graphite pillar strip directly under the fold: four equal cells, "Fast / Focused / Social / Results" at 34px wide 800, each with a red square. The first thing after the fold is a claim nobody can disagree with. — **Fix:** delete the strip. The fold's three proof lines already make the claim. If the client insists on the four words, which are in both supplied decks, reduce them to one 17px line in the path head: "Fast strategy · focused creative · social follow-up · scheduled appointments".
- **Vertical padding does all the separating** — Work, Standards and Pricing are three sections in a row on the same `#ECECE8`. They are separated by 224px gaps (styles.json `sectionGaps` 112/224/224) and a single 1px rule. C-BRIEF §3 promised a ground change every 1–2 sections and this doesn't deliver it. — **Fix:** deleting Standards (above) makes Work flow straight into Pricing. Then put the pricing section on graphite, with the white ledger panel sitting on it, and cut the gap to 112.
- **Placeholder art** — the full-bleed 1536×560 night showroom at the end of the path band. It is an AI render captioned "Illustrative image", a stock-looking dealership at blue hour. It is the second-largest image on the page and the only one that isn't customer work, on a page whose argument is "real work only". — **Fix:** delete the band. Set "Set the visit. Win the appointment." at 34px under the lane, ending the graphite section. If you want a full-bleed moment there, use a real customer frame instead (storyboard scene 4, the showroom exterior) with its customer-supplied caption.
